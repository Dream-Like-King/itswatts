# Automation Decision Checklist

Consider automating a workflow when it is:

- Repeated often or on every release
- Meaningful to users or business operations if it fails
- Stable enough to describe expected behavior clearly
- Possible to run without fragile setup or constantly changing data
- Valuable as fast feedback for a developer, tester, or release team

Consider exploratory or manual testing first when:

- The experience is changing quickly
- The risk is unclear
- Human judgment, visual quality, or discovery is the primary goal
- Setup would be more expensive to maintain than the feedback is worth
