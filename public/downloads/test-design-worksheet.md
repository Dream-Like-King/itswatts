# Test design worksheet

## 1. User goal

What is the user trying to accomplish?

## 2. Risk

What could go wrong, and what would the impact be?

## 3. Useful techniques

- **Boundaries**: just below, at, and just above a limit
- **Partitions**: representative values from valid and invalid groups
- **Decisions**: important combinations of rules and conditions
- **States**: allowed and forbidden transitions in a workflow
- **Pairwise**: useful combinations without testing every possibility

## 4. Evidence

What result, data, message, or system state would prove the behavior is correct?

## 5. Prioritize

Which checks protect the highest user, business, accessibility, or delivery risk first?
