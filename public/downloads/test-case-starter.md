# QA Test Case Starter

## Feature or story
What behavior are you testing?

## Happy path
- What does a successful outcome look like?
- What valid data and user role are required?

## Negative paths
- What happens with missing, invalid, expired, or duplicate input?
- What messages and recovery options should the user receive?

## Edge cases
- What are the boundaries, limits, retries, interruptions, and unusual states?

## Accessibility
- Can the flow be completed with a keyboard?
- Are labels, messages, focus order, and errors understandable with assistive technology?

## Integration and data
- Which API, email, permission, data state, or downstream system affects this feature?
